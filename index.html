Artist statement

Louis Picasso is an Ypsilanti native who has earned recognition in Detroit through a sound that refuses to stay in one lane. His first viral single, “COOLAX,” produced by AP, introduced his music to a growing audience and turned online attention into a real following. When he began organizing his own local shows, tickets sold out within the first two weeks.

Picasso took that momentum to the stage with his band, The Gallery, bringing live instrumentation into his performances and taking the show on the road. Performing without backing vocals, he puts his voice and the band’s musicianship at the center of the experience.

His versatility makes every release unpredictable. Melodic, harmonized rap can give way to raw, gritty garage punk, with each sound revealing another side of his artistry. Picasso follows the feeling of the music wherever it takes him, carrying that same freedom into his live shows.

You may never know what he’s dropping next, but you can count on a sonic Picasso every time.
